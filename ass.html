Segmentation, Targeting and Positioning (STP): Concepts and Importance for Small Businesses Segmentation, Targeting, and Positioning (STP) is a vital marketing tool that helps firms determine the right customers, the best market segments, and deliver their unique offer. STP allows firms to tailor their marketing efforts towards the needs, preferences, and buying behavior of the customer base as opposed to considering the market segment as a homogenous entity. Small businesses like PUF Clothing need to adopt the STP model because they often lack capital and production capabilities. With the use of STP, PUF Clothing is able to target customers with a taste for spiritually motivated fashion products. 2.1 Market Segmentation Market segmentation is the division of a market that consists of a large number of consumers into smaller market segments of consumers having similar characteristics. The market segment can be made on the basis of similar interests of consumers, purchasing behaviors, or needs. There are four main types of market segmentation. Geographic Segmentation: This type of segmentation involves classification of consumers on the basis of their geographic locations like country, region, city or town. For PUF clothing, the market segment may be identified based on the customers from Sri Lanka and later from other countries using online shopping options. Demographic Segmentation: In this type of segmentation, the customers are segmented on the basis of their demographics like age, income, occupation, education and gender. For example, the customers from which PUF clothing may benefit include young people and other customers who like distinctive T shirt designs. The income of the customers will help the company determine the pricing strategy. Psychographic Segmentation: Psychographic segmentation segments customers based on lifestyle, interests, values, attitudes, and personality traits. Such an approach is most applicable for PUF Clothing as its clothing line includes designs inspired by spirituality and universe. Thus, the brand can attract customers interested in spirituality, serenity, cosmic ideas, creativity, and individualism. Behavioral Segmentation: Behavioral segmentation segments customers depending on the way they purchase the products, use the products, demonstrate brand loyalty, and pursue certain benefits from using the product. For PUF Clothing it will be useful to find those customers who often buy graphic T shirts, follow independent brands on the Internet, or prefer wearing clothes related to their interests. In addition, the company can attract customers looking for something unique and extraordinary rather than common mass production. Market segmentation will allow PUF Clothing to learn about different consumer types attracted to its products and select those groups which have the highest potential. 2.2 Targeting The term targeting means the evaluation of various market segments and the selection of the customer groups that a business will be able to service well. Various aspects like market size, demand for products, profit margins, level of competition, accessibility of markets, and availability of resources are some of the considerations in the selection of target markets. For small companies, concentrated targeting will be beneficial since it will allow the company to concentrate its efforts towards one market rather than trying to cater to all. For instance, the initial target market for PUF Clothing would comprise fashion oriented young adults and customers with an interest in spiritual artworks, the cosmic world, and unique T shirts. Customers within this group have an interest in clothing that represents who they are and how they live their life. This will allow PUF Clothing to design and promote clothing in line with customers' interests, thus, allowing the business to spend its marketing budget in a more effective way. But this should be done after conducting market research and analyzing customers' reactions. 2.3 Positioning Positioning is the method through which a company creates a distinct image of its brand compared to competing brands in the minds of potential consumers. Positioning addresses how the business intends its customers to see its product and how they should favor its products compared to the competitive products. Good positioning can depend on product design, quality, pricing, customer satisfaction, or any other unique advantage of a brand. The PUF Clothing can be positioned as a distinct T shirt maker that integrates spiritually inspired visuals, cosmic themes, and fashion styles. The intended brand image of this firm can integrate calmness, uniqueness, and self expression. The positioning can enable the brand to effectively market its distinct image to consumers interested in significant visuals and fashion. The brand needs to consider several things to ensure its position is created properly, such as maintaining good quality design, identifiable visuals, clear communication of its brand image, and delivering value to the customers. 2.4 Importance of STP for Small Businesses STP is especially essential for small enterprises since it enables them to become more competitive in spite of having less resources compared to large companies. Firstly, STP makes it possible to allocate resources more efficiently. Not all small companies are capable of investing in massive advertising and numerous products. The process of segmenting customers will enable PUF Clothing to spend marketing budget on those consumers who can find the T shirts from the company spiritually appealing. Secondly, STP allows for product differentiation. In general, there are numerous companies operating in the clothing sector, offering T shirts designed and priced differently. PUF Clothing can differentiate itself through creating distinct corporate image of spirituality, cosmism, tranquility, and uniqueness. Thirdly, STP increases customer satisfaction. Knowing customers' needs and preferences will enable the company to design its products better, corresponding to its target consumer group. Fourth, STP will be able to promote customer loyalty because of a distinctive brand identity and consistent customer experience that will prompt purchases and recommendations. The company can develop a relationship with customers through regular communication via social media and customer feedback. Fifth, STP allows small companies to identify market niches. Through customer interest and behavior research, PUF Clothing can discover demand for certain design concepts, products or prices before making significant investments in the production of such goods. Finally, STP helps to achieve business growth. When a company identifies its identity and customers’ peculiarities, it can make decisions on product development, pricing, promotions and even market expansion. In summary, the segmentation, targeting and positioning are a helpful tool for PUF Clothing to find out their potential customers, focus on the most appropriate market niches and create an identity of the brand. Effective implementation of STP will enable PUF Clothing to utilize its limited resources effectively and make their spiritual T-shirts competitive among similar products. 3.
